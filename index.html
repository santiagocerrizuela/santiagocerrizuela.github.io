Biochemist studying how neural stem cells wake up in the healthy brain and after stroke.

Stem cell and neurobiology research
German Cancer Research Center (DKFZ), Heidelberg

Research

I want to understand what activates neural stem cells, both during normal brain homeostasis and after ischemia.

Most adult neural stem cells sit in a dormant state, and they become harder to activate with age. To find out what switches them on and how they then differentiate, I use single-cell genomics, including single-cell RNA sequencing and single-cell epigenomic profiling.

Publications

High throughput screening of novel AAV capsids identifies variants for transduction of adult NSCs within the subventricular zone

Journal and year

Adult neural stem cells could be a source for brain repair, but they are hard to modify genetically. This study screened new adeno-associated virus capsids and found variants that reach adult stem cells in the subventricular zone.
